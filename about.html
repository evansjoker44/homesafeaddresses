Why I Started HomeSafe Addresses

“I've learned that life is about taking risks, trusting the process, and believing in yourself.”

My life wasn't always what it is today. I struggled with addiction and experienced homelessness.

I've always loved the idea of working for myself, but I wanted to create something that also serves others. HomeSafe Addresses gives me the opportunity to build my own future while helping make our community a little safer.

A clearly marked address may seem like a small detail, but in an emergency, it can make a real difference. If my work helps first responders find the right home more quickly, helps protect a family, or simply gives someone greater peace of mind, then I know I'm making a positive impact.

Every curb I paint represents more than just a job. It's an opportunity to serve my community, continue growing as a person, and build something meaningful through honest work.

I believe in treating every customer with honesty, respect, and professionalism, and I'm thankful for everyone who supports my small business.

Thank you for taking the time to visit HomeSafe Addresses. I look forward to serving you.

— Shawn

Why HomeSafe Addresses?

HomeSafe Addresses exists because something as simple as a clearly marked curb address can make a meaningful difference. My goal is to make homes easier to find while providing honest, professional service to every customer.
